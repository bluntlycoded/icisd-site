About VIT Group

Founded in 1984 by the esteemed chancellor, Dr. G. Viswanathan,VIT has evolved under his visionary leadership, attaining University status in 2001. Today, VIT proudly stands with five campuses in India located in Vellore, Chennai, Amaravati (AP), Bhopal, and Bangalore, and one international campus in Mauritius. We offer an expansive suite of over 148 Undergraduate, Postgraduate, Integrated, and Research Programs, welcoming a vibrant community of more than 95,000 students from 28 Indian states and over 50 countries. Our programs are renowned for attracting a plethora of applications from across the nation and abroad, showcasing the university’s prestigious standing. VIT is renowned as the FIRST Indian institution to receive a 4 STAR Diamond rating and is distinguished among the top 550 in the QS World University Rankings by Subject in five disciplines. Furthermore, it is honored in the Top 250 universities in Asia and the Young University Rankings, alongside being ranked among the top 9 institutions in India by the prestigious Shanghai World University Rankings. In 2019, VIT was recognized as the No.1 private institution for innovation, receiving the ARIIA award from the President of India, a testament to our commitment to excellence in research and innovation. The Academic Ranking of World Universities (ARWU) Shanghai Ranking, 2021, acknowledged VIT amongst the top 10 universities in India. VIT has been conferred the title of Institute of Eminence by the Government of India in recognition of our overarching excellence in research and innovation. Our programs have earned accreditation from esteemed bodies such as ABET and the IET, affirming our commitment to global education standards. Consistently ranked among the TOP 20 Best Engineering Institutions in India from 2016-2024 by the National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF), VIT continues to set benchmarks in higher education.

About VIT-AP School of Business (VSB)

Empowering Future Leaders: The VIT-AP School of Business (VSB) stands at the forefront of educational excellence, with a visionary mission to nurture the leaders of tomorrow, capable of addressing the complex challenges and seizing the opportunities of the future. Our commitment is to develop not just business acumen but to foster an environment where leadership, ethical considerations, and a deep understanding of the socio- ecological fabric of our society are cultivated. At VSB, we recognize the indispensable role of technology in shaping the future of business. Our curriculum is meticulously crafted to integrate technological advancements, ensuring our students are well-equipped to make strategic decisions in an ever- evolving technosocioeconomic landscape. This forward-thinking approach prepares our graduates to be influential decision-makers, capable of leading with insight and integrity. In addition to a robust business education, VSB places a significant emphasis on the liberal arts, believing in the power of a wellrounded academic foundation. This unique blend empowers our students with critical thinking, effective communication skills, and an ethical compass, preparing them for diverse career paths including immediate employment, entrepreneurial ventures, and advanced studies. Our holistic educational philosophy is designed to ignite creativity, foster adaptability, and instill a global perspective in our students.     • About the University of Newcastle, Australia

    The University of Newcastle (UON) is a prestigious institution located in Australia, with campuses in Newcastle, Sydney, and Singapore. Established in 1965, it has evolved into a globally recognized university known for its commitment to innovation, research excellence, and community engagement. UON offers various undergraduate and postgraduate programs across various disciplines, including business, health, engineering, and the arts. The university prioritizes a student-centered approach, fostering a supportive learning environment. With a strong emphasis on research, UON contributes significantly to advancements in science, technology, and social issues. The university's dedication to sustainability, inclusivity, and collaboration has positioned it as a leading educational institution in Asia-Pacific. The University of Newcastle's Business School is accredited by various organizations, including EQUIS, AACSB, PRME, AMI, AHRI, ACHSM, CAUTHE, Chartered Accountants Australia and New Zealand, CPA Australia, and CFA Institute. The school's programs are recognized for their high-quality education, professional relevance, and commitment to responsible management education. The Master of Applied Finance program, which incorporates at least 70% of the CFA Program Candidate's Body of Knowledge, is also accredited by the CFA Institute. The school's commitment to quality and commitment to responsible management education is evident in its ongoing affiliations with various organizations.

    • About Heriot-Watt University, UK

    The Heriot-Watt University is a pioneering and globally recognized institution known for its innovative research, specialized expertise, and highly employable graduates. With a heritage dating back to 1821, the university has a long-standing tradition of developing transformational solutions to global challenges. Specializing in engineering, business, and science, Heriot-Watt is a leader in providing cutting-edge education tailored for the demands of a future-oriented world. The institution operates five campuses across the globe—Edinburgh, Scottish Borders, Orkney, Dubai, and Malaysia—serving a diverse community of around 27,000 students from 154 countries. Its international diversity is further highlighted by the fact that one-third of its on-campus students in Scotland hail from outside the UK, making it one of the most globally inclusive universities in the country. Heriot-Watt's achievements reflect its excellence across education and research. The university’s Watt Club, founded in 1854, holds the distinction of being the UK’s oldest graduate club and represents a thriving alumni network of over 166,000 members across 190 countries. Heriot-Watt’s research excellence is underscored by the Research Excellence Framework (REF) 2021, which ranked 86.8% of its research as world-leading or internationally excellent. The university’s global impact is demonstrated by its QS World University Ranking of 256th globally, 32nd in the UK, and 5th in Scotland for 2025, while it is also among the top 18% of universities in Europe (QS Europe Rankings 2024). Notably, Heriot-Watt excels in graduate outcomes, ranking 1st in Scotland and 2nd in the UK, with almost 90% of graduates employed or pursuing further education within 15 months of graduation (Graduate Outcomes Survey 2024).

    • About the Birmingham City University, UK

    The Birmingham City University (BCU) is a dynamic and forward-thinking institution that has been a cornerstone of higher education in the UK since its establishment in 1843. Originally founded as the Birmingham College of Art, BCU has grown into a modern, diverse university that offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs. The university is renowned for its courses in fields such as business, law, health, education, engineering, arts, and media. With a strong emphasis on practical learning and industry partnerships, BCU equips its students with the skills needed to excel in their chosen careers. Known for its inclusive and dynamic learning environment, BCU has grown into one of the largest higher education institutions in the United Kingdom, attracting students from over 100 countries worldwide. BCU is also making a mark in research and academic excellence. The university's performance in the latest Research Excellence Framework (REF) reflects its commitment to impactful research, particularly in areas such as health sciences, creative industries, and engineering. BCU's focus on applied research ensures realworld benefits, aligning with the needs of local and global industries. The university's state-of-the-art facilities, including the City Centre Campus and the STEAM house innovation hub, provide a dynamic learning environment for over 30,000 students from diverse backgrounds. This dedication to academic innovation and inclusivity positions BCU as a leading institution in the UK’s higher education landscape.
