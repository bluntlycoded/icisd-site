Call for Papers

ICISD’27 invites academicians, researchers, practitioners and industry professionals to submit original research papers, case studies, or review articles aligning with the Conference’s Theme. Submissions will undergo a rigorous peer review process to ensure high-quality content for presentation and potential publication. Paper presentation opportunities are available both online and Offline (hybrid mode).

    *Upon acceptance of the paper, it will be communicated to the corresponding authors to their registered email.

    Important Dates:

  • Full Paper Submission Deadline: December 31, 2026
  • Notification of Acceptance: January 10, 2027
  • Final Paper Submission: January 15, 2027
  • Registration Deadline: January 20, 2027
  • Conference Dates: January 29 and 30, 2027 (Hybrid)

    • Submission Guidelines:

    Articles submitted to the conference should be original contributions and should not be under consideration in any other publication at that time. Submit the manuscript as per the below submission guidelines.


  Extended Abstract Template   Full Paper Template

Manuscript Requirement Guidelines:

All the papers will undergo double-blind review and editorial processes.

Paper Submission:

Full papers adhering to the submission guidelines should be submitted using the Microsoft Conference Management Toolkit (CMT).

Submission Link

Instruction to the Authors :

1. At least one author of an accepted paper must register for the conference and present the paper in their respective tracks.
2. Maximum three authors per paper. E-certificate will be provided to all authors.
3. A conference kit will be provided to the registered participants who are presenting the paper in the physical mode.
4. Submitted manuscripts will be checked for plagiarism. It should be less than 10%. Non-adherence to this policy will result in desk rejection of paper from the conference proceedings.

Publication Opportunities:

Abstract of all accepted papers will be included in the conference proceedings with an ISBN Number, which will be published, and soft copies will be sent to the registered email ID. However, only selected papers will have the opportunity to be published in allied edited book/journals as per the norms of the respective publishers/journals.

  • Logistics (ISSN 2305-6290) (Scopus, Q1)
  • IUP Journal of Corporate Governance (C-Category in ABDC)
  • Sustainability and Climate Change (Scopus, Q3)
  • Global Business Review (Scopus, Q2, ABDC B)
  • Electronic Commerce Research (Scopus, Q1, ABDC A)
  • Global Business and Organizational Excellence (Scopus, Q1, ABDC C)
  • International Journal of Management Studies (Scopus, Q2)
  • CRC Press (Taylor & Francis Group) – Book series indexed in Scopus
